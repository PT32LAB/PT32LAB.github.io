Status key:
- **VERIFIED** -- confirmed against cited or authoritative source
- **SUPPORTED** -- consistent with known data, minor details unconfirmed
- **QUALIFIED** -- partly true but needs caveat or precision
- **UNVERIFIED** -- no source found or not independently checkable
- **STALE** -- may have been true but likely outdated
- **SOUND** -- logical argument holds under stated premises
- **PLAUSIBLE** -- reasonable but depends on assumptions
- **WEAK** -- logic has gaps or unsupported leaps
- **SPECULATIVE** -- aspirational framing presented as likely
