# Bolivia Cooperative Law: Foreign Membership + Tax Status

## Key Findings

### Foreigners CAN be cooperative members
- Ley 356 Art. 33: no nationality requirement for membership
- Constitution Art. 14(6): foreigners have same rights unless specifically restricted
- BUT: Art. 65 requires Bolivian CITIZENSHIP for council/leadership positions

### Cooperatives are NOT tax-free (except mining)
- Ley 843 Art. 37: cooperatives explicitly listed as IUE (25%) subjects
- Also subject to IVA (13%) and IT (3%)
- Mining cooperatives only: near-zero tax (~2% royalties)
- Art. 49 exemption requires never distributing to members — contradicts cooperative purpose
